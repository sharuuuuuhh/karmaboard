# Japanese Ink-Wash (Sumi-e) Art Prompts for Overview Page

Common style for all 3: Japanese ink-wash painting, soft layered misty mountains, gentle gradients, lots of empty negative space, muted greys, subtle paper texture. No text, no logos, no people, no UI elements, no borders, no watermark.

---

### 1. header-banner.webp (wide, 2400x400)
**Prompt**:
Japanese ink-wash sumi-e wide banner painting. Warm cream background (#F6F4F0). On the RIGHT third only: a soft red-orange sun circle (#E8695F, slightly textured), a five-tier pagoda on a pine-covered misty hill in front of it, faint grey mountains behind, 4 to 5 small red falling leaves/petals. The LEFT two thirds must be almost empty cream with only very faint mist so text can sit on top. Minimalist, premium, negative space, traditional Japanese aesthetic, no text, no watermark, 2400x400.

### 2. dark-card-art.webp (1200x800)
**Prompt**:
Japanese ink-wash sumi-e painting on near-black background (#16161A). Very dark charcoal misty mountains fading into the darkness, a black torii gate silhouette on a stone-step hill with dark pine trees at the RIGHT side, subtle fog. The LEFT and TOP-LEFT must stay almost pure black so white text stays readable. Low contrast, moody, serene, no text, no watermark, 1200x800.

### 3. empty-state.webp (1200x600)
**Prompt**:
Japanese ink-wash sumi-e vignette painting on off-white background (#FBFAF7). Centered: a dark ink torii gate in front of soft pale grey mountains with a faint large mountain behind it, a little grass at the base, fog fading to white at the edges. Everything fades smoothly to the background colour at all edges, minimalist, tranquil, no text, no watermark, 1200x600.
